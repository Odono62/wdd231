Local Eats Site Plan

This page outlines the structure, audience, visual direction, and layout plan for the Local Eats website. The plan is intentionally simple and can be refined as the project develops.

Site Name

Local Eats

This name was selected because it clearly communicates the focus on discovering nearby restaurants, hidden gems, and community food experiences.

Suggested domain: localeats.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of Local Eats is to help visitors discover great local restaurants, read honest reviews, explore hidden gems, and find food inspiration close to home.

  • Show featured dishes and restaurant highlights
  • Provide reviews and blog-style content
  • Help readers find food options near them

Scenarios

  • What are the best local restaurants for a casual weekend meal?
  • Where can I find honest reviews for nearby food spots?
  • How can I discover hidden restaurants that are not widely known?

Color Scheme

The color palette is warm, welcoming, and food-inspired to match the restaurant and review theme.

Primary Orange#ff6a3d
Used for buttons, links, and highlights
Accent Gold#ffb347
Used for ratings and key accents
Warm Cream#f8e8d9
Used for cards, panels, and soft backgrounds
Dark Brown#2f241f
Used for headings and body text
Light Background#fffaf7
Used for the main page background

Typography

Playfair Display

Used for headings and section titles to create a polished, editorial feel.

Inter

Used for body text, navigation, and supporting content for clear readability.

Wireframe

The home page will feature a simple responsive layout with a top navigation, a hero section, featured content, and a footer.

Small Viewport

The small-screen layout stacks sections vertically for easier reading.

Large Viewport

The large-screen layout places the featured content side by side for a wider presentation.
